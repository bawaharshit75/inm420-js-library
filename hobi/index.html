Where to go

Six places that show off Portugal, from Atlantic cliffs to sun-baked vineyards. Swipe or use the arrows.

  • City

    Lisbon

    Hilltop viewpoints, tram 28 and warm pastéis de nata straight from the oven.

  • Day trip

    Sintra

    Fairy-tale palaces and misty gardens just forty minutes from Lisbon.

  • City

    Porto

    Tiled churches, riverside cafés and port-wine cellars in Gaia.

  • Wine country

    Douro Valley

    Terraced vineyards, slow river cruises and quintas for tastings.

  • Beach

    Lagos

    Golden sea stacks, kayak-only grottos and surf breaks in the Algarve.

  • History

    Évora

    A walled Alentejo town with a Roman temple and a chapel of bones.

    When to go

    Lisbon gets around 290 sunny days a year. Summers are hot and dry; late spring and early autumn are the sweet spot for warm days and thinner crowds.

    • Best overall: May & September
    • Beach season: June to September
    • Wettest: November to January
